Privacy policy

This OAuth client is for the personal use of its owner. It is used with rclone to copy the owner's own files to the owner's Google Drive.

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared by this app.
  • File contents move directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive over TLS. They do not pass through any server operated by this app.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising are used here.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account “Third-party apps and services” page.

This page is a static document with no data collection of its own.
